# Hookster — pitch (6 slides)

1. **Title** — Hookster: copy what already went viral, write it for your business. Pay with SOL from your phone.
2. **Problem** — Small businesses and creators waste hours guessing what a Short should look like. Trend tools are desktop SaaS with monthly cards and subscriptions.
3. **Solution** — Describe your business → viral Shorts far above their channel size → why they worked → your script. 4 taps.
4. **Why Solana Mobile** — Pay-per-use micro-payments (0.01 SOL ≈ 20 hooks) with Mobile Wallet Adapter: no card, no subscription, no account — wallet is the login. Verified on-chain.
5. **How it works** — Android app (Expo + MWA) → Node server (Gemini + YouTube API) → Solana RPC payment verification.
6. **Roadmap** — Mainnet + USDC, signed-message login, Seeker dApp Store release, history of saved scripts, Telegram bot sharing the same wallet credits.
